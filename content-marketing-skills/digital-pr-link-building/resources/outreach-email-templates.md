# Digital PR & Link Outreach Email Templates

Proven, tested email outreach templates designed to maximize open and reply rates from editors, journalists, and webmasters.

---

## 1. Unlinked Brand Mention Reclamation

**Subject**: Quick note regarding your [Topic] article on [Site Name]

```text
Hi [First Name],

Read your article on [Site Name] regarding [Topic]—really appreciated your breakdown of [Specific Point].

I noticed you mentioned [Brand Name] in the section discussing [Sub-topic]. 

Would you be open to adding a quick hyperlink to our site ([URL]) so your readers can easily access the referenced tool/study?

Either way, keep up the fantastic writing!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title, Company]
```

---

## 2. Pitching an Original Data Study to Journalists

**Subject**: New Data: [Key Metric / Finding] reveals [Unexpected Trend] in [Industry]

```text
Hi [First Name],

Saw your recent coverage of [Industry/Trend] on [Publication Name]. 

We just analyzed [Dataset Description, e.g., 500,000 checkout transactions] and uncovered a surprising trend:

- [Key Finding 1]: [Surprising statistic with exact percentage]
- [Key Finding 2]: [Shift in customer behavior vs previous year]
- [Key Finding 3]: [Future prediction based on velocity]

I put together a full summary with embeddable charts here: [URL]

Happy to provide an exclusive commentary quote or additional custom cuts of the data if you're working on a story in this space.

Best,
[Your Name]
[Title, Company]
```

## 3. Broken Link / Resource Replacement Pitch

**Subject**: Broken link in your [Topic] resource guide

```text
Hi [First Name],

I was reviewing your excellent guide on [Topic] ([URL]) and found it super helpful.

Just wanted to give you a quick heads up: the link pointing to [Dead Resource Name] in section [X] seems to be returning a 404 error.

We recently published an updated, in-depth guide on [Topic] ([Your URL]) that covers [Key Unique Feature]. 

Might make a great replacement link to keep your guide pristine for your readers.

Either way, thanks for putting together such a solid resource!

Cheers,
[Your Name]
[Title, Company]
```
