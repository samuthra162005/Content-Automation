# Humanized Blog Post Templates

Use these structures for high-engagement, human-centric articles that rank well on search engines and read naturally.

---

## Template 1: The Contrarian / Myth-Busting Post

**Best for**: Thought leadership, viral shares on LinkedIn / X, establishing authority.

- **Headline Formula**: Why [Common Practice] Is Costing You [Resource / Opportunity]
- **The Cold Open**: State the myth plainly, and state your disagreement in the first 2 sentences.
- **The Evidence / The Breaking Point**: The moment or metric that proved the common wisdom is flawed.
- **The Better Framework**: 3 actionable alternatives that actually produce results.
- **The Nuance (When I Might Be Wrong)**: Acknowledging exceptions proves you aren't just sensationalizing.
- **The Next Action**: What the reader should do instead today.

---

## Template 2: The Tactical "How-To" with Zero Fluff

**Best for**: Search ranking (SEO), bookmark-worthy tutorials, practical practitioner guides.

- **Headline Formula**: How to [Achieve Specific Outcome] Without [Common Pain Point]
- **Quick Answer / TL;DR Box**: 3 bullet summary right under H1 for search snippets and busy readers.
- **Prerequisites & Tools**: What you actually need before starting (no fluff).
- **Step-by-Step Breakdown**:
  - Step 1: Specific action with visual or code snippet.
  - Step 2: "Where most people get stuck" tip.
  - Step 3: Optimization check.
- **Common Mistakes & Edge Cases**: 3 real-world errors to watch out for.
- **Conclusion**: A quick checklist or next resource.

---

## Template 3: The Deep-Dive Case Study / Breakdown

**Best for**: Product marketing, conversion, E-E-A-T proof.

- **Headline Formula**: How [Company/Person] Achieved [Result] in [Timeframe]
- **The Baseline**: Where things started (the painful status quo).
- **The Catalyst**: What triggered the decision to change.
- **The Strategy**: The exact playbook implemented.
- **The Numbers**: Authentic metrics (both positive jumps and surprising flatlines).
- **Key Lessons**: 3 portable takeaways any reader can apply to their own business.
