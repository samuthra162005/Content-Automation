# AI Cliché & Robotic Language Blacklist

When humanizing blog content, strictly purge the following patterns and replace them with natural, direct expressions.

---

## 1. Dead Giveaways (Single Words)

| AI Favorite | Why It Sounds Robotic | Human Alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Delve** | Academic, pretentious, overused in 90% of LLM outputs | Look at, explore, dig into, examine, unpack |
| **Tapestry** | Overly poetic for tech or business blogs | Mix, collection, landscape, messy reality |
| **Testament** | "A testament to..." is filler | Proof that, shows that, proves |
| **Beacon** | Hyperbolic imagery ("a beacon of hope/innovation") | Model, standard, guide |
| **Pivotal** | Overused to artificially inflate importance | Key, big, important, critical |
| **Leverage** | Corporate buzzword, impersonal | Use, tap into, take advantage of |
| **Unleash** | Marketing hyperbole ("unleash your potential") | Unlock, release, tap, start |
| **Game-changer** | Empty hype | Big shift, meaningful change, actually useful |
| **Seamless** | Rarely true in technology | Clean, easy, smooth, straightforward |
| **Bespoke** | Over-pretentious synonym for custom | Custom, tailored, built from scratch |
| **Moreover / Furthermore** | Stiff 19th-century academic transitions | And, Also, Plus, On top of that |
| **Hence / Thus** | Stiff essay phrasing | So, Because of that, That means |
| **Vibrant** | Generic adjective ("vibrant community") | Active, lively, engaged |
| **Paramount** | Formal fluff ("of paramount importance") | Crucial, must-have, matters most |

---

## 2. Banned Opening & Closing Tropes

### ❌ Banned Openers
- "In the fast-paced world of [Topic]..."
- "In today's digital age..."
- "In the ever-evolving landscape of modern business..."
- "Have you ever found yourself wondering how to [X]?"
- "It is no secret that [Topic] is essential for success."

### ✅ Human Openers
- Direct statement: *"Most advice about keyword research in 2026 is wrong."*
- Anecdote / Hook: *"Three months ago, our organic traffic dropped 40% overnight. Here's what Google changed—and how we recovered."*
- Contrarian premise: *"Stop writing 3,000-word blog posts if 400 words can answer the user's question."*

---

### ❌ Banned Conclusions
- "In conclusion, [Topic] is a journey, not a destination."
- "To wrap things up..."
- "All in all, by following these tips, you'll be well on your way..."
- "As we look ahead to the future..."

### ✅ Human Conclusions
- A punchy final takeaway or challenge: *"Pick one tactic from this list, test it on your lowest-performing article this Tuesday, and check your metrics in two weeks."*
- A memorable parting thought: *"Write for the human reader first. The algorithms will follow."*
