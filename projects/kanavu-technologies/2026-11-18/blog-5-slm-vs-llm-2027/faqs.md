# Frequently Asked Questions: SLM vs LLM in 2027: Key Differences, Benefits and Use Cases

### FAQ 1: What is the primary difference between an SLM and an LLM?
**Answer (22 words):** SLMs have smaller parameter sizes allowing local on-device execution, whereas LLMs possess hundreds of billions of parameters requiring expensive cloud data centers.

### FAQ 2: Can a Small Language Model match the accuracy of a Large Language Model?
**Answer (21 words):** Yes, when fine-tuned on curated industry data or paired with retrieval pipelines, SLMs frequently outperform general LLMs on specialized corporate tasks.

### FAQ 3: Why are regulated industries choosing Small Language Models?
**Answer (19 words):** Regulated industries deploy SLMs locally on private internal servers, ensuring confidential financial and healthcare data never leaves corporate firewalls.
