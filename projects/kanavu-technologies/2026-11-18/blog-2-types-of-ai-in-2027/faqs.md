# Frequently Asked Questions: Types of AI in 2027: Uses and Real-World Examples

### FAQ 1: What are the main categories of artificial intelligence used by businesses?
**Answer (19 words):** Enterprises primarily deploy reactive automation systems, limited memory predictive analytics, multimodal generative foundation models, and autonomous goal-directed software agents.

### FAQ 2: How does an autonomous AI agent differ from a standard chatbot?
**Answer (22 words):** Autonomous agents can break down complex objectives, call external software APIs, query corporate databases, and complete multi-step tasks independently without human guidance.

### FAQ 3: Which business sector experiences the fastest return on AI adoption?
**Answer (21 words):** Customer support, supply chain logistics, and software development teams experience rapid financial returns through automated ticketing, predictive routing, and accelerated testing.
