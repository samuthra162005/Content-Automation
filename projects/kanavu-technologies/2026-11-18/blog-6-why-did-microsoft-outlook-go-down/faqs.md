# Frequently Asked Questions: Why Did Microsoft Outlook Go Down? The Global Outage Explained

### FAQ 1: What was the root cause of the Microsoft Outlook outage?
**Answer (22 words):** The outage was caused by a wide area network routing update that misdirected traffic and overwhelmed authentication proxies across regional edge servers.

### FAQ 2: Was the Microsoft Outlook disruption caused by a cybersecurity breach?
**Answer (21 words):** No, official post-incident reports confirmed the disruption was entirely an internal networking routing configuration issue, not a malicious external cyber attack.

### FAQ 3: How can enterprises maintain communication during major cloud email outages?
**Answer (21 words):** Organizations should implement secondary MX record spooling, independent backup webmail services, and out-of-band communication channels to maintain operational continuity during outages.
