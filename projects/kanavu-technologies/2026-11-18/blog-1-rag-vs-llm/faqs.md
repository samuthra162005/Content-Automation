# Frequently Asked Questions: RAG vs LLM: Key Differences, Benefits and Real-World Use Cases

### FAQ 1: What is the main advantage of RAG over a traditional LLM?
**Answer (20 words):** RAG retrieves verified company data before answering questions, preventing hallucinations and providing transparent source citations without requiring expensive model retraining.

### FAQ 2: Does RAG require fine-tuning the underlying foundation model?
**Answer (21 words):** No, RAG injects retrieved context directly into the prompt at runtime, allowing businesses to use standard models without modifying neural parameters.

### FAQ 3: How frequently can internal documents be updated in a RAG system?
**Answer (19 words):** Documents can be re-indexed instantly within the vector database, giving the system access to newly updated enterprise records immediately.
