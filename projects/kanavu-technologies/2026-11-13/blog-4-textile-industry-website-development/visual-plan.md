# Visual Architecture Plan: Website Development for Textile Industry

## 1. Editorial Rationale & Subject Alignment
* **Topic:** B2B Digital Textile Catalog Portal
* **Visual Concept:** Minimal editorial UI mockup of a modern textile B2B portal interface on a solid white background (`#FFFFFF`). Showcasing fabric swatches (woven cotton, linen twill), technical GSM and yarn count filter badges, and an intuitive wholesale inquiry action card.
* **Canvas Style:** Clean white background (`#FFFFFF`), ample whitespace, modern enterprise SaaS aesthetic.
* **Colors:** Deep Slate (`#0F172A`), Warm Terracotta / Sand (`#C2410C`), Tech Blue (`#2563EB`), Natural Linen White (`#F8FAFC`).
