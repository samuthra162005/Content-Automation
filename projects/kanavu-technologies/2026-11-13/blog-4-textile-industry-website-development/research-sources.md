# Research Summary & Sources: Website Development for Textile Industry

## 1. Search Intent & Industry Context
* **Primary Query:** `Website Development for Textile Industry`
* **Intent:** Commercial B2B Implementation & Architecture.
* **Industry Context:** Textile manufacturing (spinning, weaving, dyeing, garment export) is historically offline or reliant on PDF catalogs. B2B textile buyers in Europe, the US, and domestic wholesale hubs require digital self-service swatch sampling, certified GSM/weave specifications, and tiered wholesale pricing.

---

## 2. Analyzed Reliable Sources
1. **Ministry of Textiles, Government of India (`texmin.nic.in`):** Export benchmarks, digital trade facilitation initiatives, and technical textile classifications.
2. **India Brand Equity Foundation (IBEF) Textile Report:** Digital adoption trends showing 40% growth in direct-from-mill B2B international procurement.
3. **Texvalley Erode Trade Ecosystem:** Operational workflows for wholesale fabric inquiries, minimum order quantities (MOQs), and swatch approval lifecycles.
4. **B2B E-Commerce Standards (Figma & Shopify Plus B2B Benchmarks):** Gated wholesale pricing, custom quotation workflows (RFQ), and enterprise ERP connectors (SAP, Tally, Zoho Inventory).

---

## 3. Competitor Gaps Bridged
* **Beyond Generic E-Commerce:** Generic web agencies pitch standard retail stores (Shopify consumer carts). A true textile mill needs swatch sample ordering, minimum order quantities (MOQs in meters/kilograms), and GSM-based technical filtering that generic retail templates lack.
