# Visual Plan: What Is an AI Agent and How Does It Work?

## 1. Visual Strategy
* **Overall Goal:** Provide two distinct, technical, content-matched illustrations that ground autonomous software concepts into clean, scannable mental models.
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen landscape (`1536x864` or `1792x1024`).
* **Background:** Solid crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with ample whitespace and zero 3D visual noise.
* **Canvas Typography:** Large bold header titles embedded directly on the image canvas in clean sans-serif typography.
