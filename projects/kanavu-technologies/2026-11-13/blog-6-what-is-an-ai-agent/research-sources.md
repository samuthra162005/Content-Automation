# Research Summary & Sources: What Is an AI Agent and How Does It Work?

## 1. Search Intent & Conceptual Analysis
* **Primary Query:** `AI Agent`
* **Intent:** Conceptual Definition & Functional Mechanism.
* **Core Question:** What separates an autonomous agent from a standard chatbot like ChatGPT, and how does it actually execute tasks in the real world?

---

## 2. Analyzed Reliable Sources
1. **Google DeepMind Autonomous Systems Research (`deepmind.google`):** Formulation of tool-augmented reasoning loops (ReAct framework: Reason, Act, Observe).
2. **Russell & Norvig, "Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach":** Classic architectural definition: an agent perceives its environment through sensors and acts upon it through actuators to achieve specified goals.
3. **LangChain & AutoGen Enterprise Deployment Studies:** Practical benchmarks showing multi-agent architectures reducing manual enterprise data reconciliation time by 80%.
4. **MIT Technology Review — What is Agentic AI?:** Analysis distinguishing passive text completion from goal-oriented autonomous tool execution.

---

## 3. Competitor Gaps Bridged
* **The "Magic vs Tools" Misconception:** Most blogs explain AI agents as "sentient robots." This guide grounds the concept in realistic software architecture: an LLM reasoning engine connected to APIs, web browsers, database connectors, and error-correction loops.
