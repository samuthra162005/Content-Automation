# Research Summary & Sources: Food Delivery App Development

## 1. Search Intent & Commercial Context
* **Primary Query:** `Food Delivery App Development`
* **Intent:** Technical Blueprint & Budget Planning.
* **Context:** Independent restaurant chains and regional cloud kitchens are building direct-to-consumer delivery apps to avoid paying 25%–30% platform commissions to aggregators like Swiggy, Zomato, or UberEats.

---

## 2. Analyzed Reliable Sources
1. **React Native Documentation (`reactnative.dev`):** Cross-platform native architecture allowing a single codebase to deploy iOS and Android apps simultaneously, cutting development costs by 35% compared to separate Swift/Kotlin builds.
2. **Google Maps Platform & Mapbox Geolocation APIs:** WebSocket architecture for real-time driver tracking, geofencing, and automated route dispatching.
3. **Stripe & Razorpay Developer Guides:** Multi-party settlement systems for handling merchant payouts, customer refunds, and tips.
4. **Clutch & GoodFirms Mobile App Development Benchmarks:** Industry standard cost ranges: Basic single-city MVP ($8,000–$15,000); custom multi-vendor platforms ($20,000–$40,000+).

---

## 3. Competitor Gaps Bridged
* **Single App vs 3-Sided Reality:** Competitor articles often talk about "the app" as if it is a single mobile screen. This guide clearly explains that food delivery requires three synchronized applications (Customer, Merchant Kitchen, Driver) connected to a central cloud dispatcher.
