# Visual Plan: How to Build a Food Delivery App

## 1. Visual Strategy
* **Overall Goal:** Provide two distinct, technical diagrams illustrating the three-sided connected delivery architecture (customer, restaurant, driver) and an architectural cost/tier comparison graphic.
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen landscape (`1536x864` or `1792x1024`).
* **Background:** Solid crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) with ample whitespace.
* **Canvas Typography:** Large bold header titles embedded directly on the image canvas in clean sans-serif typography.
