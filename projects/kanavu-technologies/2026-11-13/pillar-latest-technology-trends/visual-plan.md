# Visual Plan: Latest Technology Trends

## 1. Visual Strategy Overview
* **Objective:** Visually ground complex architectural and paradigm shifts in clear, high-contrast, informative diagrams.
* **Canvas Style:** Clean pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background, 16:9 aspect ratio (`1920x1080`), corporate slate and cobalt palette, sans-serif typography.
* **Bold Title Mandate:** Every image has a prominent, high-contrast headline rendered directly on the canvas image file.
