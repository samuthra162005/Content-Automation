# Research Summary & Sources: Technology Trends in 2027

## 1. Search Intent & Strategic Analysis
* **Primary Query:** `Technology Trends in 2027`
* **Intent:** Technical Forward-Looking Strategy & Architecture.
* **Audience:** Engineering leads and business decision-makers looking past generic AI hype to identify concrete, production-ready software shifts.

---

## 2. Analyzed Reliable Sources
1. **W3C WebAssembly Working Group (`w3.org/wasm`):** Standardizing WASI (WebAssembly System Interface) allowing sandboxed, ultra-lightweight binary execution across cloud servers, displacing traditional micro-VM overhead.
2. **Gartner Top Strategic Technology Trends Report:** Forecasts over 70% of enterprise software development utilizing multi-agent AI verification pipelines by 2027.
3. **ACM & IEEE Software Engineering Publications:** Studies on edge-native serverless runtimes achieving sub-10ms global cold starts.
4. **Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF):** Evolution of cloud computing trends from heavy Kubernetes container orchestrations to modular WASI runtimes.

## 3. Competitor Gaps Bridged
* **Hype vs Production Reality:** Competitors write vague sci-fi predictions ("AI will replace all developers"). This article focuses on tangible engineering shifts: agentic review pipelines, WASM portability, and edge-native databases that businesses are actually implementing.
