# Brand Guidelines & Content Architecture: Kanavu Technologies

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Brand Name:** Kanavu Technologies
- **Official Website:** `https://tech.kanavu.org`
- **Location:** Erode, Tamil Nadu, India (serving regional, national, and global enterprise clients)
- **Mission:** Enterprise software engineering, cloud architecture, modern web & mobile app development, and technical workforce training.
- **Tone of Voice:** Authoritative, practical, accessible, and grounded. Educational value must lead every piece of content.

---

## 2. Special Rules for Kanavu Technologies Content (Master Prompt Section 8)
1. **The Blog Is NOT About Kanavu Technologies:** The main subject must dominate the article (90%+ educational depth). Kanavu is an enabler that supports the reader's next step, not an interruption.
2. **One Concise Brand Connection Paragraph:** Place the client connection in a single, natural paragraph near the latter part of the article (after delivering substantial value).
3. **No Unsubstantiated Superlatives:** Never describe Kanavu Technologies as "the best IT company", "the number-one company", or "the leading software company" without verifiable independent proof.
4. **Natural Lead-ins for Pillar Links:** When linking to the pillar hub, construct a natural lead-in sentence connecting the specific topic to the broader technology landscape.
5. **Verified Service Mapping:** Connect only to verified service pages that genuinely match the article topic:
   - Web & Cloud Infrastructure: `https://tech.kanavu.org/services/web-development`
   - Mobile Application Development: `https://tech.kanavu.org/services/mobile-app-development`
   - Custom Software Engineering & AI: `https://tech.kanavu.org/services/custom-software`
   - CMS & B2B Portals: `https://tech.kanavu.org/services/cms-development`
6. **No Repetitive Text:** Never paste identical promotional paragraphs across multiple articles.

## 4. Visual & Illustration Standards (Sections 14 & 15)
- **Canvas & Dimensions:** 16:9 widescreen format (`1536x864` or `1792x1024`).
- **Background:** Solid crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) or very soft neutral (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Typography on Canvas:** Large bold title inside every explanatory infographic in clean sans-serif; readable high-contrast labels.
- **Color Palette:** Tech Blue (`#2563EB`), Indigo (`#4F46E5`), Emerald Green (`#059669`), Slate (`#334155`), and Amber (`#EA580C`).
- **Composition:** 2 to 3 distinct visual diagrams distributed contextually across the article (Process flows, side-by-side comparisons, operational loops). Zero decorative clutter, zero stock photo clichés.
