# Qivera Digital Visual Guidelines (V7 Engine)

## 1. Visual Composition & Canvas Principles
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels).
* **Background Tone:** Clean pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with soft neutral/light-blue card accents (`#F0F9FF` or `#F8FAFC`).
* **Title Typography on Canvas:**
  - Font Size: 40–44pt bold (`Segoe UI`).
  - Title Color: Deep Slate Navy (`#0F172A`).
  - Subtitle Color: Vibrant Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`) or Emerald Green (`#10B981`).
* **Canvas Density:** Full canvas utilization with balanced margins; zero empty waste.

---

## 2. Signature Visual Elements
* **Visual Categories:** `EDUCATIONAL_INFOGRAPHIC`, `MULTI_CARD_GRID`, and `PROCESS_WORKFLOW`.
* **LexiRef Visuals:** Vocabulary flashcards, word relationship etymology trees (Prefix-Root-Suffix), contextual reading loops, spaced repetition schedules.
* **HabitShare Visuals:** Habit streak calendars, micro-habit trigger-routine loops, 2-minute rule decision trees, peer accountability networks.
* **Prohibited Visuals:** Fake app download counts, cartoon clip art, robotic mannequins, garish neon colors.
