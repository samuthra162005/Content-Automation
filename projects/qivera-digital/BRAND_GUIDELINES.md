# Qivera Digital Brand Guidelines

## 1. Practice Identity
* **Brand Name:** Qivera Digital (Qivera Digital Ventures LLC)
* **Domain / Hub URL:** `https://qiveradigital.com/`
* **Specialty:** Mobile Utility, Reference Applications & Productivity Software (LexiRef, HabitShare, Unitly, SudokuQuest)
* **Target Audience:** Language learners, students, professionals, reading enthusiasts, self-improvement seekers
* **Brand Tone:** Encouraging, pragmatic, intellectually curious, accessible, evidence-backed

---

## 2. Core Strategic Principles
* **Practical Everyday Utility:** Prioritize lightweight, privacy-conscious utility without bloated subscription models or data harvesting.
* **LexiRef Product Discipline:** Focus on verified vocabulary-building techniques (etymology root systems, spaced repetition, contextual reading, active recall) and offline accessibility.
* **HabitShare Product Discipline:** Focus on sustainable habit formation (cue-routine-reward loops, friction reduction, micro-habits, friend accountability) without exaggerated behavioral claims.
* **Understated Commercial Connection:** 90%+ educational value. Brand/app connection is 1 concise paragraph near the end. Zero unsubstantiated superlatives (*"best dictionary in the world"*, *"guaranteed memory cure"*).
