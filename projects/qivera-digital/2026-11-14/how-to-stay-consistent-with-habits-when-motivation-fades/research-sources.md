# Research Sources: Habit Formation & Cognitive Consistency

## 1. Primary References
1. **Fogg, B.J. (2019). *Tiny Habits: The Small Changes That Change Everything*. Houghton Mifflin Harcourt.**
   * Finding: Behavior happens when Motivation, Ability, and a Prompt converge ($B = MAP$). When motivation is near zero, ability must be made exceptionally easy (friction near zero).
2. **Clear, James (2018). *Atomic Habits*. Avery / Penguin.**
   * Finding: The "Never Miss Twice" rule prevents psychological defeatism; environment design beats willpower in 85%+ of long-term consistency trials.
3. **American Society of Training and Development (ASTD) Study on Accountability:**
   * Finding: The probability of completing a goal increases to 65% with a specific commitment, and surges to 95% when committing to a specific accountability partner with regular check-ins.
4. **HabitShare App Architecture:**
   * Features verified: Social habit tracking with custom privacy per habit, shared friend streaks, encouragement messaging, and reminder notifications without gamified clutter.
