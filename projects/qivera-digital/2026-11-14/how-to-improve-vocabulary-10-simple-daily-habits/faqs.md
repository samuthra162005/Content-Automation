# Frequently Asked Questions: Vocabulary Improvement

## Question 1: How many new words should I learn every day?
**Answer:** Focusing on learning three to five new words daily with contextual sentences ensures durable long-term retention without causing cognitive overload.
* **Word Count:** 20 words (Target: 15–25 words) — **PASSED**

---

## Question 2: Why do I forget new words shortly after memorizing them?
**Answer:** Words fade quickly when memorized in isolation without active conversational usage, writing practice, or spaced repetition to reinforce neural pathways.
* **Word Count:** 20 words (Target: 15–25 words) — **PASSED**

---

## Question 3: Does reading fiction really expand adult vocabulary effectively?
**Answer:** Yes, reading fiction exposes you to descriptive adjectives, figurative metaphors, and varied conversational dialogue rarely found in standard business writing.
* **Word Count:** 20 words (Target: 15–25 words) — **PASSED**
