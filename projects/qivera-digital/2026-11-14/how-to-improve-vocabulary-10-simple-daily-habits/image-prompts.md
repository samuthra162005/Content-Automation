# V7 Structured Image Prompts: Vocabulary Improvement (Qivera Digital)

## Figure 1: 10 Daily Habits to Expand Your Vocabulary (Hero Infographic)

### 14-Field Structured Internal Design Brief
1. **Brand:** Qivera Digital
2. **Blog Title:** How to Improve Vocabulary: 10 Simple Daily Habits That Actually Work
3. **H2 Section / Location:** 10 Practical Habits for Lifelong Word Mastery (Figure 1 - Hero Visual)
4. **Target Audience:** Language learners, students, book lovers, and career professionals.
5. **Image Purpose:** Provide an inspiring, educational visual overview of the 10 micro-habits that expand active vocabulary.
6. **Selected Visual Type:** `EDUCATIONAL_INFOGRAPHIC` (Subtype: `MULTI_CARD_GRID`)
7. **Selection Rationale:** The section explains 10 distinct daily habits; displaying them as organized, icon-supported cards allows readers to scan and adopt habits immediately.
8. **Realistic Photography Required:** No.
9. **Illustration / Infographic Required:** Yes (Clean editorial infographic).
10. **Exact Product or Screenshot Assets Required:** No.
11. **Core Concept to Illustrate:** 10 actionable daily habits categorized into Reading, Root Analysis, Sentence Writing, Conversation, and Spaced Review.
12. **Key Elements to Include:**
    - Large bold on-canvas headline: `10 DAILY HABITS TO EXPAND YOUR VOCABULARY`
    - Subtitle: `Practical Routines for Contextual Reading, Word Decoding & Active Retention`
    - 4 balanced category modules (Reading & Listening, Structural Etymology, Active Production, Memory Consolidation).
    - Clear icons (Book, Tree Root, Journal Pen, Flashcard Clock).
13. **Visual Style and Composition Notes:**
    - Canvas Background: Solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with soft blue/slate card containers (`#F0F9FF`).
    - Palette: Royal Cobalt Blue (`#2563EB`), Emerald Green (`#10B981`), Amber (`#F59E0B`), Slate 900 (`#0F172A`).
    - Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (`1920 × 1080` pixels), balanced margins, high typographic contrast.
14. **Specialized Prompt:**
    ```text
    A high-resolution 16:9 widescreen educational infographic illustrating "10 Daily Habits to Expand Your Vocabulary".
    Headline: Large bold on-canvas typography "10 DAILY HABITS TO EXPAND YOUR VOCABULARY" at top center in dark slate (#0F172A).
    Subtitle: "Practical Routines for Contextual Reading, Word Decoding & Active Retention" in vibrant blue (#2563EB).
    Layout: 4 structured rounded cards displaying the core habits:
    - Card 1: Contextual Reading & Listening (Diverse essays, fiction, thought-provoking podcasts).
    - Card 2: Structural Root Analysis (Greek & Latin prefixes, morphological root trees).
    - Card 3: Active Production (Sentence journals, spoken recaps, daily conversations).
    - Card 4: Memory Reinforcement (Spaced intervals, synonym substitution, bedtime review).
    Visual Style: Modern editorial tech illustration, crisp typography, pure white canvas background, 1920x1080 resolution.
    ```

## Figure 2: The 4-Stage Vocabulary Retention Loop

### 14-Field Structured Internal Design Brief
1. **Brand:** Qivera Digital
2. **Blog Title:** How to Improve Vocabulary: 10 Simple Daily Habits That Actually Work
3. **H2 Section / Location:** Daily Vocabulary Learning Habits Comparison (Figure 2)
4. **Target Audience:** Language learners curious about the cognitive psychology of word acquisition.
5. **Image Purpose:** Illustrate the cyclical progression from passive word discovery to fluent conversational usage.
6. **Selected Visual Type:** `PROCESS_WORKFLOW` (Subtype: `CIRCULAR_RETENTION_LOOP`)
7. **Selection Rationale:** A cyclical workflow clearly depicts how words move through memory stages without falling into the forgetting curve.
8. **Realistic Photography Required:** No.
9. **Illustration / Infographic Required:** Yes.
10. **Exact Product or Screenshot Assets Required:** No.
11. **Core Concept to Illustrate:** 4 stages: 1. Encounter in Context → 2. Decode Root & Meaning → 3. Record in Sentence Journal → 4. Active Spaced Retrieval.
12. **Key Elements to Include:**
    - Large bold on-canvas headline: `THE 4-STAGE VOCABULARY RETENTION LOOP`
    - Subtitle: `How Words Transition from Passive Recognition to Permanent Fluency`
    - 4 interconnected circular or rounded nodes connected by directional flow arrows.
    - Central icon representing long-term cognitive memory.
13. **Visual Style and Composition Notes:**
    - Clean pure white canvas background (`#FFFFFF`).
    - High-contrast nodes with emerald green, cobalt blue, violet, and amber color coding.
    - 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080`), crisp vector lines, large legible labels.
14. **Specialized Prompt:**
    ```text
    A professional 16:9 process diagram illustrating "The 4-Stage Vocabulary Retention Loop".
    Headline: Bold on-canvas typography "THE 4-STAGE VOCABULARY RETENTION LOOP" at top center.
    Subtitle: "How Words Transition from Passive Recognition to Permanent Fluency" in emerald green (#10B981).
    Workflow: 4 sequential connected circular stages forming a continuous learning loop:
    - Stage 1: Encounter in Context (Reading literature & articles).
    - Stage 2: Decode Root & Meaning (Etymology, prefixes & dictionary lookup).
    - Stage 3: Record in Sentence Journal (Contextual writing & personal usage).
    - Stage 4: Active Spaced Retrieval (Review intervals & daily speech).
    Style: Clean modern vector infographic, balanced composition, pure white background, 1920x1080 resolution.
    ```
