# Research Sources: Cognitive Science & Vocabulary Acquisition

## 1. Primary References
1. **Nation, I.S.P. (2001). *Learning Vocabulary in Another Language*. Cambridge University Press.**
   * Finding: Incidental vocabulary learning through extensive reading accounts for the majority of adult word acquisition, provided unknown words appear in comprehensible contexts.
2. **Ebbinghaus Forgetting Curve & Spaced Retrieval Practice:**
   * Finding: Memory retention of new vocabulary decays rapidly within 48 hours unless reinforced through spaced intervals (1 day, 3 days, 7 days, 14 days).
3. **Morphological Awareness in Lexical Development (Bowers & Kirby, 2010):**
   * Finding: Learning Greek and Latin prefixes, roots, and suffixes allows learners to infer the meanings of over 60% of unfamiliar academic and professional English words.
4. **Qivera Digital Product Specifications:**
   * App: LexiRef. Features verified: Completely offline comprehensive dictionary and thesaurus, root word search, custom study lists, and privacy-focused architecture without invasive tracking.
