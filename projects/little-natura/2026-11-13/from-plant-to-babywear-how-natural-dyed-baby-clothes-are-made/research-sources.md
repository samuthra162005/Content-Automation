# Research Sources & Fact-Checking Report

## 1. Verified Sources & Standards

1. **Global Organic Textile Standard (GOTS) Version 7.0 (2023)**
   - *Verification Scope:* Section 5.2.2 prohibiting synthetic azo colorants releasing carcinogenic arylamines, halogenated solvents, and heavy metal mordants (chrome, copper, lead).
   - *Fact Verified:* Natural dyeing processes must utilize non-toxic mineral mordants (potassium alum, natural vegetable tannins) to satisfy organic certification without heavy metal residues.

2. **OEKO-TEX® Standard 100 — Annex 4 (Product Class I: Baby & Toddler Items)**
   - *Verification Scope:* Limit values for infants (<36 months). Strictest thresholds for extractable heavy metals, formaldehyde (<16 ppm), and pH range matching neonatal skin (4.0–7.5).
   - *Fact Verified:* Baby apparel requires gentle processing free from harsh industrial resins and chemical fixatives.

3. **Pediatric Dermatology & Neonatal Skin Physiology**
   - *Verification Scope:* Epidermal thickness and transepidermal permeability in infants.
   - *Fact Verified:* Infant stratum corneum is roughly 30% thinner than adult skin, increasing vulnerability to mechanical friction and chemical contact dermatitis.
   - *Correction Applied:* Strictly avoided claiming clothing "cures" eczema, prevents rashes, or is completely safe for teething chewing. Instead, accurately described the fabric as breathable, soft, and free from synthetic plasticizers.

4. **Botanical Textile Extraction & Dyeing Mechanisms**
   - *Verification Scope:* *Curcuma longa* (turmeric), *Indigofera tinctoria* (indigo), *Rubia cordifolia* (madder), and *Punica granatum* (pomegranate).
   - *Fact Verified:* Water decoctions at 60°C–80°C preserve botanical pigments without scorching fibers; myrobalan tannins prime cellulose fibers for lasting color affinity without chemical synthetic fixatives.
