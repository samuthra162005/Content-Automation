# Frequently Asked Questions: Natural-Dyed Baby Clothes

## FAQ 1
* **Question:** What plant materials are used to dye natural baby clothes?
* **Answer:** Artisans use dried botanical sources such as turmeric rhizomes, fermented indigo leaves, madder roots, pomegranate rinds, and marigold petals for color.
