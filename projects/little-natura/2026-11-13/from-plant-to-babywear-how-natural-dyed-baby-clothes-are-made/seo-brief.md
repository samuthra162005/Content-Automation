# SEO Content Brief: Natural-Dyed Baby Clothes

## 1. Core Blog Identifiers
* **Working Title:** From Plant to Babywear: How Natural-Dyed Baby Clothes Are Made?
* **Client Brand:** Little Natura (`https://littlenatura.com`)
* **Target Audience:** New parents, expectant mothers, mindful gift shoppers, pediatric caregivers, and eco-conscious families.
* **Content Archetype:** Step-by-Step Production Process & Ingredient Comparison Guide.
* **Search Intent:** Informational & Commercial Investigation (Parents seeking to understand how botanical baby garments are crafted and why they are safer than chemically dyed alternatives).

---

## 2. Keyword Strategy
* **Primary Keyword:** `Natural-Dyed Baby Clothes` (Search volume targeted for sustainable babywear and infant eczema searches).
* **Secondary Keywords:**
  - `Plant-based dyes for babywear`
  - `Organic baby clothes`
  - `Ayurvedic herbal dyed baby clothing`
  - `Non-toxic baby clothes`
  - `Chemical-free infant apparel`
* **LSI & Semantic Vocabulary:**
  - *Botanical extraction, cold-water mordanting, myrobalan tannins, GOTS-certified organic cotton, skin allergen prevention, turmeric curcumin, indigo leaf, azo-free infant clothing.*

---

## 3. Reader Problem & Search Solution
* **The Problem:** Parents are alarmed by contact allergies, chemical rashes, and chemical residues from synthetic dyes (azo dyes, heavy metal fixatives, formaldehyde) on delicate newborn skin, whose epidermal barrier is 30% thinner than adult skin.
* **The Solution:** A transparent, grounded breakdown of how pure plants (turmeric, indigo, madder, marigold) are transformed into vibrant, skin-safe infant garments using non-toxic natural mordants and organic cotton.

---

## 4. Editorial Voice & Structural Plan
* **Voice:** Warm, human, nurturing, authoritative, and scientifically accurate.
* **Word Count Target:** Exactly 450–500 words for the main body (excluding metadata, captions, and FAQs).
* **Opening Hook:** Direct answer to the title question in sentence one. Zero generic openers.
* **Visual Plan:** Two 16:9 infographics (1920×1080) with bold headlines rendered on canvas.
* **Brand Placement:** Exactly 1 concise paragraph near the end connecting to Little Natura's collection.
