# Visual Plan: Natural-Dyed Baby Clothes

## 1. Visual Strategy Overview
* **Brand:** Little Natura (`https://littlenatura.com`)
* **Design Philosophy:** Warm, organic, tactile, and scientifically transparent. Clean warm cream background (`#FFFDF7`), soft oatmeal card containers, and earthy botanical accents (sage green `#819B7A`, terracotta `#C25E40`, turmeric yellow `#E3A857`, and indigo `#455A64`).
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels).
* **On-Canvas Typography:** High-contrast dark earth brown title (`#432818`) with amber subtitle (`#B45309`).
