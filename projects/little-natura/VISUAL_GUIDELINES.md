# Little Natura Visual Guidelines

## 1. Visual Identity & Canvas Principles
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels).
* **Background Tone:** Clean warm cream (`#FFFDF7`) or crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with soft oatmeal (`#F5EFEB`) and gentle sage (`#E8EFE9`) card accents.
* **Canvas Density:** Full canvas utilization with generous yet balanced margins; avoid huge empty voids and do not shrink illustrations to tiny centered icons.
* **Headline Standard:** Bold, large typography rendered **directly inside the image canvas**:
  - Title Font Size: 42–46pt bold, high-contrast dark charcoal/terracotta (`#432818` or `#1F2937`).
  - Subtitle Font Size: 18–22pt regular, warm amber/botanical green (`#B45309` or `#059669`).

---

## 2. Brand Visual Elements
* **Textures & Materials:** Real woven organic cotton textures, gentle folds, soft infant onesies, wooden buttons, raw botanical bundles (dried madder roots, fresh turmeric rhizomes, marigold flowers, indigo vats).
* **Color Palette:**
  - Cream Background: `#FFFDF7`
  - Terracotta Red/Peach: `#C25E40` / `#E07A5F`
  - Botanical Sage Green: `#819B7A` / `#059669`
  - Warm Turmeric Yellow: `#E3A857` / `#D97706`
  - Herbal Indigo Blue: `#455A64` / `#2C5282`
  - Text & Accents: Deep Earth Brown (`#432818`)
* **Strictly Prohibited:**
  - Plastic synthetic shine, neon saturated synthetic colors.
  - Creepy robotic baby dolls, stiff corporate mannequin graphics.
  - Cluttered 3D tech icons or generic business diagrams.
