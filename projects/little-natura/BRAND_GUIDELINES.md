# Little Natura Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity & Purpose
* **Brand Name:** Little Natura
* **Website Domain:** `https://littlenatura.com`
* **Industry:** Sustainable Infant Apparel, Organic Baby Clothing & Ayurvastra Herbal Wear
* **Mission:** Protecting delicate newborn skin by crafting infant apparel from 100% GOTS-certified organic cotton dyed exclusively with botanical extracts, therapeutic herbs, and non-toxic minerals.

---

## 2. Target Audience
* **Primary Audience:** Expectant parents, new mothers and fathers, eco-conscious families seeking safe, chemical-free clothing for newborns and toddlers.
* **Specialized Segment:** Parents of babies with eczema, contact dermatitis, sensitive skin, or sensory sensitivities.
* **Gifting Segment:** Grandparents, friends, and baby shower shoppers looking for thoughtful, ethical, premium infant gifts.

---

## 3. Core Botanical Palette & Ingredients
* **Turmeric (*Curcuma longa*):** Warm golden yellow; renowned for natural antimicrobial properties.
* **Indigo (*Indigofera tinctoria*):** Soothing natural blue; derived from fermented leaves.
* **Madder Root (*Rubia cordifolia* / Manjistha):** Earthy terracotta and soft peach; calming and skin-friendly.
* **Pomegranate Rind (*Punica granatum*):** Warm sage, khaki, and soft yellow; rich in natural protective tannins.
* **Marigold (*Tagetes*):** Cheerful soft yellow-orange; gentle and sun-fast.
* **Natural Mordants:** Only food-grade potassium alum and tannin-rich myrobalan (*Terminalia chebula*). Never heavy metal salts (chrome, copper, zinc).

---

## 4. Voice, Tone & Writing Style
* **Tone:** Warm, nurturing, informative, transparent, reassuring, and scientifically grounded.
* **Key Vocabulary:** Breathable, botanical infusion, gentle on skin, hypoallergenic, unbleached organic cotton, natural washfastness, earth-friendly.
* **Strictly Prohibited:**
  - Banned AI clichés (*"In today's fast-paced world...", "Imagine...", "delve", "testament", "tapestry"*).
  - Unsubstantiated marketing superlatives (*"best baby clothing brand in the world"*, *"unbeatable purity"*, *"number one"*).
  - Fear-mongering about conventional clothing (state chemical facts accurately and calmly without sensationalism).

---

## 5. Blog & SEO Standards
* **Body Word Count:** Exactly 450–500 words for branch blogs (counted programmatically, excluding metadata, captions, and FAQs).
* **First Sentence:** Answers the title question immediately and clearly.
* **Brand Mention:** Restricted to 1 concise, natural connection paragraph near the conclusion.
* **FAQs:** Exactly 3 concise FAQs (15–25 words per answer).
* **4-Tier Internal Linking:**
  1. Pillar Hub: Natural/Organic Baby Clothing Guide (`https://littlenatura.com/blogs/journal/organic-baby-clothing-guide`)
  2. Related Branch Blog: Caring for Natural-Dyed Clothes (`https://littlenatura.com/blogs/journal/how-to-care-for-natural-dyed-baby-clothes`)
  3. Verified Service/Product Page: Little Natura Newborn Collection (`https://littlenatura.com/collections/natural-dyed-baby-clothes`)
  4. External Authority Citation: GOTS (`https://global-standard.org/`) or OEKO-TEX Standard 100.
