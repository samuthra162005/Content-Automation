# Frequently Asked Questions: Types of Braces for Kids

## FAQ 1
* **Question:** What is the ideal age for a child's first braces consultation?
* **Answer:** The American Association of Orthodontists recommends every child receive an initial orthodontic screening by age seven to monitor jaw growth and erupting teeth.
* **Word Count:** 23 words (Target: 15–25 words)
* **Status:** PASS

## FAQ 2
* **Question:** Can children choose colorful bands on their braces?
* **Answer:** Yes, traditional metal and self-ligating braces allow children to personalize their smile with bright rubber band colors at every routine adjustment visit.
* **Word Count:** 22 words (Target: 15–25 words)
* **Status:** PASS

## FAQ 3
* **Question:** Do braces hurt when first placed on a child's teeth?
* **Answer:** Children feel mild pressure and soreness for three to five days after placement, which quickly subsides with soft foods and cold rinses.
* **Word Count:** 22 words (Target: 15–25 words)
* **Status:** PASS
