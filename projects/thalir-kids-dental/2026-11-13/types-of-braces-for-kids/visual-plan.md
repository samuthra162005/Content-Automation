# Visual Plan: Types of Braces for Kids

## 1. Visual Strategy Overview
* **Practice Brand:** Thalir Kids Speciality Dental Clinic, Erode (`https://thalirkidsdental.com`)
* **Design Aesthetic:** Friendly, cheerful, clinically accurate, and parent-reassuring.
* **Canvas Specifications:** 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels).
* **Color Palette:** Pure clinical white background (`#FFFFFF`), soft pastel mint cards (`#E6F4EA`), vibrant teal accents (`#0D9488`), and deep slate navy typography (`#0F172A`).
* **Signature Elements:** Chompy mascot (friendly, brave cartoon tooth), anatomically accurate pediatric dental arches, colorful bracket elastics, and smooth transparent aligner trays.

## 2. Planned Image Assets

### Figure 1: 4-Type Pediatric Braces Comparison Grid
* **Filename:** `hero-image.jpg`
* **Placement:** Immediately after Section 1 (`The Right Braces for Your Child`).
* **Visual Archetype:** `MULTI_CARD_GRID` (4-card balanced grid).
* **On-Canvas Headline:** `TYPES OF BRACES FOR KIDS: A COMPLETE PARENT GUIDE`
* **On-Canvas Subtitle:** `Comparing Traditional Metal, Ceramic, Self-Ligating & Clear Aligners`
* **Core Cards:**
  1. *Traditional Metal:* Stainless steel brackets with colorful blue/purple rubber elastics.
  2. *Ceramic Brackets:* Translucent tooth-colored brackets blending smoothly with natural enamel.
  3. *Self-Ligating Braces:* Modern low-friction metal slide clips without rubber bands.
  4. *Clear Removable Aligners:* Smooth transparent orthodontic aligner tray.
  *Featuring Chompy mascot smiling proudly beside the guide.*
* **Alt Text:** *Comprehensive infographic comparing metal braces, ceramic brackets, self-ligating braces, and clear aligners for children.*
