In Tamil Nadu, parents call this **Ethu Pal**, known clinically as protruding teeth or overjet.

While many assume kids outgrow it, protruding teeth affect biting, speech, and dental safety.

### [HTML: <H2>]
## What Triggers Ethu Pal in Young Children?

Teeth do not shift on their own; protrusion stems from childhood oral habits or jaw growth patterns.

#### Clinical Reference Table: Oral Habits & Dental Impact

| Habit / Factor | Dental Impact | Ideal Age |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thumb Sucking** | Pushes teeth outward | Ages 3–5 |
| **Mouth Breathing** | Causes narrow arch | Ages 4–7 |
| **Tongue Thrust** | Tilts front teeth | Ages 5–8 |
| **Genetics** | Underdeveloped lower jaw | By Age 7 |

Signs parents can spot at home:
* Upper front teeth resting visibly over the lower lip.
* Habitual mouth breathing or noisy breathing while asleep.
* Difficulty biting cleanly through foods like apples or dosas.

### [HTML: <H2>]
## Why Early Attention Matters for Protruding Front Teeth

Protruding front teeth lose the natural cushioning of the lips.

During active play, unprotected teeth face three times higher risk of chipping or fall injuries.

Official [NHS guidance on orthodontic treatments and protruding teeth](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/braces-and-orthodontics/) confirms prominent teeth face serious injury risks that early appliances intercept.

Left unaddressed, abnormal bites force muscle compensation, worsening speech clarity and jaw alignment.

### [HTML: <H2>]
## Frequently Asked Questions About Ethu Pal (Protruding Teeth)

* **[HTML: <H3>] Q1: At what age should parents consult a pediatric dentist about protruding teeth?**  
  **Clinical Answer:** Age seven is the ideal benchmark. By this age, the first permanent molars and incisors have erupted, allowing the pediatric dentist to evaluate and guide jaw growth while bones remain adaptable.

* **[HTML: <H3>] Q2: Can protruding milk teeth correct themselves without treatment?**  
  **Clinical Answer:** Mild shifting may improve if childhood oral habits stop before age four. However, if the upper jaw has narrowed or habits persist, spontaneous correction is rare and requires guided pediatric care.

* **[HTML: <H3>] Q3: Why does thumb sucking cause Ethu Pal in young children?**  
  **Clinical Answer:** Thumb sucking applies continuous outward leverage against upper front teeth while pulling lower teeth inward, gradually narrowing the dental arch and creating an exaggerated forward overjet.

* **[HTML: <H3>] Q4: Will my child definitely need permanent teeth extracted for braces later?**  
  **Clinical Answer:** Not necessarily. Early interceptive treatment gently expands narrow jaws and corrects oral habits, creating sufficient space for incoming permanent teeth and significantly reducing the need for future extractions.

* **[HTML: <H3>] Q5: What is the typical duration of early preventive orthodontic treatment?**  
  **Clinical Answer:** Early interceptive treatments—such as habit-breaking appliances or gentle expanders—typically last 6 to 12 months. Afterwards, the pediatric dentist monitors natural tooth eruption as the child grows.
