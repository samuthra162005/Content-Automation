# Thalir Kids Dental Visual Guidelines

## 1. Visual Composition & Canvas Principles
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels).
* **Background Tone:** Clean pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft pastel mint (`#E6F4EA`).
* **Title Typography on Canvas:**
  - Font Size: 42–46pt bold (`Segoe UI`).
  - Title Color: Deep slate navy (`#0F172A`).
  - Subtitle Color: Vibrant clinical teal (`#0D9488`).
* **Canvas Density:** Full canvas utilization with clean margins; zero empty waste.

---

## 2. Signature Visual Elements
* **Mascot Integration:** Chompy (friendly smiling tooth character giving thumbs-up or holding a soft toothbrush).
* **Anatomical Accuracy:** Accurate pediatric dental arch with primary and mixed dentition (central incisors, canines, primary molars).
* **Child-Friendly Details:** Colorful elastomeric rubber bands (blue, purple, teal, green), smooth ceramic brackets, clear removable aligners.
* **Prohibited Visuals:** Scary surgical extraction forceps, bloody needles, intimidating mechanical pliers, dull dark corporate business charts.
