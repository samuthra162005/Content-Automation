# Kanavu Digital Visual Guidelines (V5 Engine)

## 1. Visual Composition & Canvas Principles
* **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels).
* **Background Tone:** Clean pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle cool gray card accents (`#F8FAFC`).
* **Title Typography on Canvas:**
  - Font Size: 42–46pt bold (`Segoe UI`).
  - Title Color: Google Search Blue (`#1A73E8`) or Slate 900 (`#0F172A`).
  - Subtitle Color: Emerald Green (`#34A853` or `#059669`).
* **Canvas Density:** Full canvas utilization with balanced margins; zero empty waste.

---

## 2. Signature Visual Elements
* **Visual Category:** `EDUCATIONAL_INFOGRAPHIC` and `TECHNICAL_DIAGRAM`
* **Elements:** Search engine result layouts, multi-channel growth funnels (SEO $\rightarrow$ PPC $\rightarrow$ Social $\rightarrow$ CRO), conversion analytics bars, local map pin search ranking indicators.
* **Prohibited Visuals:** Generic business handshakes, fake dashboard graphs showing 10,000% ROI, cartoon currency stacks, cluttered 3D floating icons.
