# Visual Plan: Best Digital Marketing Agency in Erode | Kanavu Digital

## 1. Executive Visual Strategy
* **Visual Philosophy:** Educational, data-driven, and commercially clear. Infographics must directly unpack strategic marketing concepts without relying on generic stock photos, artificial 3D clutter, or unsubstantiated revenue claims.
* **Aspect Ratio:** Strictly 16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels).
* **Canvas Integration:** Full canvas utilization with clean pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), generous margins, on-canvas bold headers, and high-contrast typographic hierarchy.
