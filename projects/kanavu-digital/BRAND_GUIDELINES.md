# Kanavu Digital Marketing Brand Guidelines

## 1. Practice Identity
* **Brand Name:** Kanavu Digital Marketing
* **Domain / Hub URL:** `https://tech.kanavu.org/blogs/digital/`
* **Specialty:** Growth Marketing, Search Engine Optimization (SEO), Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Performance Advertising & Local Business Marketing
* **Location Focus:** Erode, Western Tamil Nadu & Global Digital Solutions
* **Target Audience:** Business owners, textile manufacturers, hospital directors, retail enterprises, and marketing leaders seeking transparent, measurable digital revenue growth.

---

## 2. Core Strategic Principles
* **Evidence-Based Marketing:** Strategy grounded in Google Search Central fundamentals, transparent analytical dashboards, and conversion-rate optimization (CRO).
* **Zero Ranking Guarantees:** Strictly avoid guaranteeing #1 Google rankings, overnight virality, or instant AI Overview citations. Emphasize sustainable organic authority and verified search intent.
* **Understated Commercial Tone:** Deliver 90%+ educational and strategic value on what distinguishes a high-performing agency (custom attribution, localized technical SEO, multi-channel synergy).
* **Tamil Nadu Regional Context:** Acknowledge the dynamic industrial ecosystem of Erode (textiles, healthcare, agriculture, educational institutions, retail showrooms) and how local search optimization drives verified customer footfall and inquiries.

---

## 3. Editorial & Factual Rules
* **No Unsubstantiated Superlatives:** Avoid *"number one marketing agency in the world"* or *"unrivaled guarantees"*. Focus on verified methodologies, technical rigor, and holistic performance.
* **Word Count Mandate:** Main body strictly **450–500 words** (programmatically measured, excluding metadata, captions, and FAQs).
* **FAQs:** Exactly 3 concise FAQs (15–25 words per answer).
* **First Sentence:** Answers the title question immediately and clearly.
