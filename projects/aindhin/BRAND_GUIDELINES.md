# Aindhin Brand Guidelines

## 1. Practice Identity
* **Brand Name:** Aindhin
* **Domain / Hub URL:** `https://aindhin.com/`
* **Specialty:** Career-Focused Data Analytics Certification, SQL Training, Business Intelligence (Power BI & Tableau), Python & Career Transitioning
* **Target Audience:** College students, early-career software developers, business analysts, non-tech career transitioners across India and global learners.
* **Brand Tone:** Educational, structured, rigorous, encouraging, career-focused.

---

## 2. Core Strategic Principles
* **Hands-on Technical Rigor:** Provide syntactically accurate SQL code examples (SELECT, FROM, WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING, JOIN), logical query execution orders, and clean relational schema models.
* **Balanced BI Tool Evaluation:** Objectively compare Power BI and Tableau across data modeling, DAX vs calculated fields, licensing costs, corporate adoption, and learning curve without claiming universal superiority.
* **Career-Oriented Guidance:** Connect technical query mechanics and BI dashboards to real-world job responsibilities (e-commerce sales reporting, customer churn tracking, healthcare patient analytics).
* **Understated Commercial Connection:** 90%+ educational value. Connect to Aindhin's certified Data Analytics Course naturally in 1 concise paragraph near the end.
