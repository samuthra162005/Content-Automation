Select a primary keyword that:

- Reflects actual search intent
- Is relevant to the client's audience
- Fits the content naturally
- Avoids unnecessary competition with existing pages

Preserve user-approved keywords.

## 10. Supporting Keyword Research

Identify:

- Three to five relevant secondary keywords
- Useful long-tail variations
- Common question queries
- Relevant entities and synonyms
- Natural language variations

Classify keyword intent: Informational, Navigational, Commercial Investigation, Transactional, or Local.

## 11. Search Volume and Difficulty

Use actual keyword data only when available from an authorized source.

Distinguish measured data, third-party estimates, editorial judgment, and unknown metrics.

Never invent monthly search volumes, CPC numbers, keyword difficulty percentages, or traffic forecasts.

## 12. Search Intent Alignment

Directly satisfy the reader's primary task:

- "How to improve vocabulary" → Actionable vocabulary-building methods and daily reading habits.
- "Power BI vs Tableau" → Fair, balanced comparison of data modeling, visualization, cost, and learning curve.
- "SQL for beginners" → Clear, working query examples (SELECT, WHERE, JOIN) and practical explanations.

Never replace the expected educational answer with a generic sales pitch.

# PART 5 — TOPIC CLUSTERS, MONTHLY ROADMAPS & CANNIBALIZATION

## 13. Understand Existing Content

Compare the proposed title with already published client content to identify:

- Exact duplicates
- Closely overlapping topics
- Similar search intent
- Existing pillar pages and spoke articles

## 14. Monthly Pillar & Branch Topic Cluster Architecture

Establish a clear monthly topical hierarchy for the client:

* **Pillar Hub Blog:** Comprehensive, authoritative guide addressing the broad core theme (e.g., *Complete Data Analytics Career Roadmap* or *Mastering English Communication*).
* **Branch Spoke Blogs:** Focused, high-intent supporting articles exploring specific subtopics (e.g., *SQL vs Python for Data Beginners*, *Power BI vs Tableau*, *Daily Phonics Practice Guide*).
* **Cross-Linking Protocol:** Branch blogs naturally link up to the Pillar Hub; Pillar Hubs systematically link out to supporting branches.

## 15. Content Cannibalization Review

If the proposed blog substantially overlaps an existing published article, recommend updating, consolidating, or establishing a genuinely distinct angle, and document this in the topic cluster report.

# PART 6 — VERIFIED INTERNAL LINKING

## 16. Discover Real Internal URLs

Collect verified URLs from website navigation, sitemaps, service pages, and existing blogs.

Never manufacture URLs or link to unverified slugs.

## 17. Select Contextual Links

Integrate up to 4 distinct link tiers where verified pages exist:

- **Tier A (Pillar Hub Link):** Broader category authority context.
- **Tier B (Supporting Branch Link):** Closely related supporting article.
- **Tier C (Commercial Service / Product Link):** Logical next step for consultation or inquiry.
- **Tier D (External Authority Citation):** Official standards, documentation, or government sources.

## 18. Link Placement & Narrative Flow Rules

- **Strict Prohibition in Hook / Intro:** Never place links in the opening hook sentence or opening paragraph (readers must receive immediate, unhindered value and a direct answer before navigating).
- **Smooth Narrative Flow:** Maintain natural connective tissue between paragraphs and H2/H3 sections so the article reads as a cohesive, engaging guide rather than isolated bullet points.
- **Even Distribution:** Distribute internal links evenly across the middle analytical sections inside meaningful explanatory sentences with descriptive anchor text.
- **Single CTA in Conclusion:** Limit the conclusion section to at most ONE natural, contextual brand/service CTA link; never stack or cluster multiple links in the final section.
- **Strict Prohibition in FAQs:** Never place links inside or after the FAQ section.
- **No Table or Caption Links:** Never place links inside tables or figure captions.

## 19. Link Validation & Reporting

Test all URLs, follow permitted redirects, confirm contextual relevance, and document verification results in a dedicated linking report.

# PART 8 — AEO: ANSWER ENGINE OPTIMIZATION

## 22. Direct Answer First & Front Hook Flow

The opening sentence must directly answer the title question without preamble or clichés (*"In today's fast-paced world..."*, *"Imagine..."*, *"Let's delve into..."*). The entire opening paragraph must serve as a pure, link-free direct answer hook that bridges smoothly into the core problem or framework. Section transitions must flow logically without jarring leaps. Conclusions must synthesize actionable strategic takeaways with a single, natural brand takeaway.

## 23. Extractable Explanations & Concise FAQs

- Provide clear definitions, structured steps, and side-by-side comparative matrices suitable for answer extraction.
- Include exactly three concise FAQs (strictly 15–25 words per answer).
- Never repeat main body explanations word-for-word in the FAQs.

## 32. 16:9 Image Quality Standards
- Aspect ratio: **16:9 widescreen (`1920 × 1080` pixels)**.
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`), soft neutral (`#F8FAFC`), or deep obsidian (`#0A0F1D`) background with balanced margins.
- **Large bold on-canvas typography AT THE TOP**: 40–44pt bold title positioned strictly at the top of the canvas (`Y: 0` to `Y: 245`), paired with 18–22pt subtitle and vibrant category badge pill.
- **Strict Content-Topic Relevance**: Every visual must authentically depict its specific article domain (e.g. digital marketing architectures, customer signal vaults, ad bidding models; never recycled software CI/CD deployment or unrelated engineering diagrams).
- **Style Diversity Across Cluster**: Seamlessly combine 2D vector process blueprints, 3D isometric systems, realistic studio/cinematic photography, and technical war room dashboards.
- Rich custom AI editorial illustrations composited with high-fidelity vector styling. No flat empty programmer wireframe boxes.
- Mandatory programmatic inspection via `generators/audit_visual_quality.js`.
